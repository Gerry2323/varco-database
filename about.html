Project information

About the Database

The VARCO Materials Database is an engineering decision-support resource developed to organize and compare material properties, supplier products, manufacturing methods, source documents, and experimental coating results for lunar infrastructure research.

The project focuses on vacuum plasma spray, cold spray, regolith–ceramic composites, metallic substrates, feedstock powders, and the evidence needed to evaluate candidate coating systems.

What the Website Provides

Materials Catalog

Search and review manual entries and imported spreadsheet records.

Engineering Comparison

Filter materials, compare reported properties, and explore Ashby-style charts.

Process Screening

Estimate preliminary cold-spray critical velocities with transparent assumptions and uncertainty.

Data and Engineering Notice

Database values may come from manufacturer datasheets, supplier catalogs, handbooks, research articles, standards, experimental results, or clearly identified proxy sources. “Not reported” means that a value was not available from the referenced source.

Calculations and charts are intended for preliminary research screening. Important values must be verified against the linked source and confirmed through appropriate testing before they are used for equipment selection, process qualification, or safety-critical decisions.
